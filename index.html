          The lesson-authoring guide — the app instructs, the AI creates
          # resource: olt://guide/lesson-authoring (read by your AI)

1. Plans are SHORT — 3 to 7 days, never more. Iterate weekly.
2. A lesson is 10–20 min and always flows:
   warm-up recall → new vocab (≤5 words) → the words in context
   (story or dialogue) → active practice (conversation) →
   flashcard check → call complete_lesson with notes.
3. In voice mode: have the learner SAY every new word aloud.
   Flashcards by voice: prompt the word, learner translates.
4. Weight topics by profile goals; pull real themes for the
   "news" topic from the language's home country.
5. End every week by reviewing results and writing next
   week's plan with create_plan.
